Ten strategies, reimplemented in-browser: Character, Recursive Character, Token-based, Markdown Headers, Sentence Window, Paragraph, Semantic (approximated via word-overlap), Sliding Window, Parent-Child, and Late Chunking (conceptual). Mirrors LangChain splitters where they exist (CharacterTextSplitter, RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter, TokenTextSplitter, MarkdownHeaderTextSplitter) and approximates the rest for teaching purposes — not the library itself.
